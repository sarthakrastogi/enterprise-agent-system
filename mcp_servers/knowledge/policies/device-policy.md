# Laptop and Device Policy

- Every employee is issued one company laptop, replaced every 4 years or when it fails.
- Personal devices (BYOD) may access email and chat through the managed mobile app only. Personal laptops must not access internal systems.
- Laptops must have full-disk encryption, automatic updates and the endpoint protection agent enabled. Do not disable them.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours so the device can be wiped remotely.
- Peripherals (monitors, keyboards, headsets) up to $300 can be ordered through a helpdesk ticket without manager approval.
