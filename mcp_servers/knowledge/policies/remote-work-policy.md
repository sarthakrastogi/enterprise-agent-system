# Remote Work and Network Policy

- Connect to the VPN for any internal system that is not published through single sign-on.
- Public Wi-Fi is allowed only with the VPN connected.
- Working from another country for more than 14 days needs approval from your manager and the security team, because of data-residency rules.
- Do not print confidential documents at home or in public places.
- Video calls about confidential matters must not be taken in public spaces.
